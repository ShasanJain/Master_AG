# Piece Brief: The Younger Dryas (Earth's Lost Cataclysm)

## Concept
A 50-second 9:16 vertical animated documentary investigating the catastrophic Younger Dryas event (~12,800 to 11,600 BP). When Earth was thawing out of the Last Glacial Maximum, a fragmented comet impact plunged the northern hemisphere back into deep glacial freeze, wiped out North American megafauna, caused catastrophic megaflooding, and catalyzed the birth of human agriculture and megalithic stone architecture at Göbekli Tepe.

2. **The Comet Airburst (7.2 - 14.4s)**: Fragmented comet detonation, atmospheric shockwaves, molten spherules.

4. **The Megafloods (21.6 - 28.8s)**: Glacial Lake Agassiz breach, surging floodwaters, Atlantic conveyor halt.

6. **The Thaw & Göbekli Tepe (36.0 - 43.2s)**: Sudden +10°C snap, wild wheat sprouting, colossal T-Pillar 43 ("Vulture Stone").
7. **Epilogue / Coda (43.2 - 50.0s)**: From cataclysm to civilization, celestial memory, endless loop bridge.
