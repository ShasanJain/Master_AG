| Claim | Fact & Source |

| **Market Surge** | Over 50% of bookings for Toyota Innova Hycross and Urban Cruiser Hyryder are strong hybrids. (Source: Toyota Kirloskar Motor / Autopunditz FY24-25). |
| **City Fuel Economy** | Certified mileage reaches 27.97 km/L (Maruti Grand Vitara / Toyota Hyryder) via dual engine + electric regenerative motor. (Source: ARAI official certification). |
| **Engine-Off Time** | In urban stop-and-go traffic, strong hybrids operate in pure EV mode up to 50–60% of drive time without external charging. (Source: Toyota Technical Review / ARAI testing). |
